## Visibility, reverse playback and closure

Validate finite rigid world-to-camera poses: last row [0,0,0,1], determinant +1, orthogonality tolerance 1e-5; K finite, positive focal lengths, invertible, native size consistent. Reject malformed inputs. Positive finite depth and alpha >=0.5 required for motion evidence. Source-to-target projection uses official per-frame K/w2c, foreground depth; valid iff in bounds, target alpha>=0.5 and absolute depth discrepancy <=max(0.02*z,0.01). Depth test also rejects samples behind a foreground occluder; newly revealed pixels use current structure. Invalid transport is reported, not credited as stability.

Candidate builds a canonical frame-keyed atlas (sorted original frame keys), then renders in requested order. Reverse recomputation from reverse-presented inputs must produce identical per-key geometry/texture/identity, rather than hiding causal hysteresis. Full forward/reverse/ping-pong videos: forward 33, reverse 33, ping-pong [0..32,31..0] 65 frames. Canonical atlas gives zero edit/texture drift on revisits; this property is partly by design and is not evidence of physical tracking. Original arcs are open: do not invent a 32→0 smooth loop. Closure measures the return to frame0 in ping-pong; real closed-camera synthetic fixture separately verifies closure.
