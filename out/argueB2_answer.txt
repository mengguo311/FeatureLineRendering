The one method-critical question (Q2/Q3) was decided by DIRECT EVIDENCE from the plys, not opinion:

## Q2/Q3 — 2DGS ply vs 3DGS ply: is the OVERALL score byte-identical?  ->  NO.
Hard evidence:
- src/common.py load_gaussians() reads scale_0, scale_1, scale_2 (3 axes) and derives
  normal = column of R with smallest scale (shortest-covariance axis of a 3D ellipsoid).
- The crown chair/lego carriers are 3DGS plys: cglib/outputs/chair_static/point_cloud.ply
  fields = ['scale_0','scale_1','scale_2']  (3 scales, confirmed).
- The Truck 2DGS ply (out/2dgs_truck/.../point_cloud.ply) fields = ['scale_0','scale_1'] ONLY
  (2 scales; a flat disk with an explicit disk normal). scale_2 is ABSENT.
=> The shipped load_gaussians CANNOT run byte-identically on a 2DGS ply (missing scale_2;
   normal semantics differ: shortest-of-3-covariance-axis vs explicit disk normal).
   Feeding a 2DGS ply would be a SILENT METHOD CHANGE. Rejected.

## Decision (pre-registered): train VANILLA 3DGS on Truck, matching the crown pipeline exactly.
- This makes the method path byte-identical to chair/lego (same 3-scale ply, same load_gaussians,
  same OVERALL score, same M1b, same temporal harness). Only the DATA SOURCE (real photos +
  COLMAP estimated poses) changes — which is exactly what B is designed to test.
- The 2DGS Truck ckpt (PSNR 25.09) is RETAINED as a by-product but is NOT the carrier for B.
- No 3DGS trainer was on dss9; cloned graphdeco-inria/gaussian-splatting into ext/, pinned to
  commit 472689c (2-value rasterizer API + no `antialiasing` kwarg) to match the installed
  diff_gaussian_rasterization 0.0.0 build; one data-plumbing patch to render() to unpack the
  4-tuple (color,radii,depth,alpha) the installed CUDA kernel returns. NO method/recipe change.
